### General notes
- **Weather-dependent stops** (Mount Wellington summit, Wineglass Bay lookout, Cape Tourville) — build in flexibility to swap for an indoor/lower-effort activity if conditions turn.
- **Restaurants to book ahead:** Cascade Brewery Bar (if used), your Day 3 group dinner for 11, Sealife Bicheno, Black Cow Bistro, Hellyers Restaurant (Cradle Mountain — books out early), Hamer's Bar & Bistro, and Blue Social Eatery for New Year's Eve.
